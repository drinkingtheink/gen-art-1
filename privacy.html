Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

Nothing about you is collected, stored or shared. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. The site makes no requests to anyone else — even the typeface and the favicon are part of the page rather than fetched from somewhere.

What stays in your browser

Pieces are generated on your own machine and never leave it. Exporting an SVG, a PNG or a CSS rule happens in the browser and saves to your device; nothing is uploaded.

Nothing is written to cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage or any other browser storage. Close the tab and no trace of your session remains. The consequence is worth knowing: a piece you do not export or copy a link to is gone.

The copy buttons write to your clipboard, and only when you press them.

What the server sees

The site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps ordinary web server logs — IP address, browser, time, the address requested — as any host does. Those logs are Netlify's and are handled under their privacy policy. They are not used to build any profile of you and nothing is added to them here.

One thing follows from how permalinks work. A piece lives entirely in its URL, so when you load or share a link the parameters of that piece — generator, seed, palette, every dial — are part of the address and appear in those logs like any other URL. They describe the artwork, not you.

Link previews

When a shared link is pasted somewhere that unfurls URLs, that service fetches a preview image from this site. The image is redrawn from the parameters in the link each time and is never stored; there is no database here and no record of who asked for what. The service doing the unfurling is governed by its own policy, not this one.

Images from elsewhere

The room photographs behind See it on a wall come from Unsplash, but they are served from this site rather than loaded from theirs — so looking at a room tells Unsplash nothing, and your browser contacts no one but this domain.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction and erasure apply to personal data that has been collected. None has been, so there is nothing held here to access, correct or delete.

Changes

If this ever stops being true — if anything is added that stores or measures something — this page changes first, and the date under the title says when.
