That covers the output, not the machine. The generators, the interface and the rest of the source are the author's work; what you may do with the code itself is whatever the repository says.

Because every piece is a pure function of its parameters, two people who arrive at the same seed and the same dials get the same picture. Nothing here grants you exclusivity over a piece, only the right to use what you made.

The room photographs

See it on a wall hangs your piece in a photograph of a real room. Those photographs are not ours. They come from their photographers via Unsplash, are credited under each room, and are here so you can judge a piece at size — not for you to publish. Take away the artwork; leave the room behind.
